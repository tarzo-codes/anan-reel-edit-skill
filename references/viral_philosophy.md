## 0. The one-line philosophy: HOOK HARD, EDIT LEAN, EARN TRUST

Heavy design pays in **the first ~1.5 s** and on **moments that explain a mechanism** (a chart, a number, a comparison). Everywhere else restraint scores better. In a Bangla market full of fake signal pages and deepfakes, polish alone no longer reads as credible: a **custom, explanatory, clearly human** edit does. The goal is the Bangla trading reel people **forward to warn a friend**.

---

## 1. What every platform actually ranks on (2025-2026)

All four platforms score the same three things:
1. **Not swiped away in the first ~3 s** (Instagram "skip rate" = left within 3 s; Shorts "viewed vs swiped away").
2. **Watch time / completion** (Facebook judges completion relative to length, so padding hurts).
3. **Sends / shares** (Mosseri: sends weigh most with non-followers; Meta's Reels models predict shares to WhatsApp / Messenger).

Then: **originality**. Facebook (Jul 2025), Instagram (Apr 2026), YouTube (Oct 2026) and TikTok all cut reach for reposts, templates, and third-party watermarks. Editing effort is NOT punished (fact-check: "unedited reels beat edited" = Mostly False; in the one direct test edited posts got ~2x reach).

**Platform priority for Anan (Bangladesh ad reach, DataReportal late 2025):** Facebook 64.0M > TikTok 56.2M (+27 %/yr) > YouTube 49.8M > Instagram 9.15M. Edit for **Facebook Reels + TikTok first**, Shorts as a funnel to long-form, Instagram last.

## 2. Trust & compliance come first (a violation removes distribution, whatever the edit)

- **Never** on screen, in speech or caption: guaranteed / risk-free returns, "X taka in 24 hours", "double your money", get-rich-quick. Meta's fraud standard (organic posts too) bans using **past returns to suggest future ones** -> no P&L screenshot, balance, or profit counter as "proof you can do it". Past trades = **lessons, include the losers**.
- No live DSE price calls, no forex / crypto broker or P2P promotion (Bangladesh Bank restricts both; BSEC flagged fake stock-tip pages Sept 2025).
- Scam-awareness / exposé content is **explicitly protected** by Meta -> a strong lane for Anan.
- Burn in a short **risk line** (Bangla + English) during explanations; "education, not a call" framing; real name and face.
- TikTok: switch on the **commercial disclosure** toggle whenever a reel promotes his own course / Telegram community.
- **No AI presenter / AI voice**, no realistic AI images of people or institutions (deepfake-scam context; YouTube demonetises AI finance personas).

## 3. Hooks (`0.0 s – 3.0 s`)

**Timing (well evidenced):** Anan's voice is mid-sentence by ~0.3 s; the payoff promise is **on-screen text from frame 1**; the frame **moves** from frame 1. No intro, logo sting, fade-in or greeting. (Speech within 3 s beat music-only opens: +24.7 % retention at 10 s, Emplifi.) About half of viewers watch muted -> the text must carry the hook alone.

**Rotate four hook families, never the same treatment twice in a row in a batch:**

### Hook A: Disillusionment / Myth-bust (Umar Ashraf style)
- Attack a retail belief: *"ক্যান্ডেলস্টিক মুখস্থ করে জীবনেও প্রফিটেবল হতে পারবা না।"*
- Visual: headline BEHIND the RVM cut-out (`V7`) + 1-2 tilted glass cards. RGB split / zoom blur allowed here but not on every reel.

### Hook B: Specific number / Risk:Reward math (TradeZella / TJR style)
- Anchor with a precise number that **teaches**, not flexes: `1 : 4 RR`, `৯৫% ট্রেডার ৬ মাসে অ্যাকাউন্ট উড়ায়`, "risk 1 % to make 4 %".
- **Retired:** the `+$500 = ৳60,000` profit counter (implies future returns from past results).
- Visual: `mono_hero` ticker pill counting on an S-curve.

### Hook C: Loss story / Downfall reversal
- *"ট্রেডিং শুরু করার পর হঠাৎ করে তোমার ডাউনফল শুরু হবে..."* / "my biggest mistake".
- Visual: moody grade, crimson `লস` / `DOWNFALL` with 12fps posterised jitter, one sub-bass drop.
- Cold-open variant: lift the strongest 2 s line from the middle to `0.0 s` with a match-zoom whip cut if the raw starts slowly.

### Hook D: Anti-scam exposé (new, strongest local lane)
- Signal groups, fake BSEC / DSE pages, "guaranteed profit" Telegram channels, deepfake investment ads: *"এই টেলিগ্রাম সিগন্যাল গ্রুপগুলো কিভাবে তোমার টাকা খায়..."*.
- Visual: blurred / redacted screenshot on a glass card + red stamp; never show a real person's identifiable account.

**Face:** open on Anan in close-up or medium, always with motion + text, never a static selfie frame. A/B test face-first vs graphic-first (evidence is mixed).

## 4. The 5-beat retention architecture (master cut **40-60 s**)

```
[0.0s – 2.5s]   BEAT 1: THE STOP-SCROLL HOOK  (heaviest design of the reel)
                - Zero dead air; voice by 0.3 s; hook text from frame 1; motion from frame 1.
                - One hook family from section 3, treatment rotated per reel.

[2.5s – 10s]    BEAT 2: SHATTER THE ILLUSION (THE PROBLEM)
                - Razor cuts, no pauses or retakes.
                - Visual change every 2.5-4 s (punch-in, graphic, B-roll or layout shift -
                  not necessarily a hard cut). No sub-1-second cutting.

[10s – 24s]     BEAT 3: THE MECHANISM (SHOW, DON'T TELL)
                - Every 3D plane, glass card, chart move must EXPLAIN something
                  (LiquiditySweepChart, RiskRewardBox 1:4, VsCards, 3D canvas).
                - Anan's face visible for most of the reel (PiP badge during long breakdowns).
                - Drop random stock and decorative effects.

[24s – 38s]     BEAT 4: THE TRUTH BEAT
                - HOLD 4-5 s calmly on the core truth (breathing room scores better here).
                - Audio stop-drop at most ONCE per reel, here: music to -inf 0.4 s before the
                  line, land it dry, slam back with a 2-step camera push.

[38s – End]     BEAT 5: SEND / SAVE CTA + LOOP
                - Lead with "এটা তোমার ট্রেডার বন্ধুকে পাঠাও" (send) or save; comment second.
                - Telegram CTA allowed only clearly separate from signals/calls, and
                  disclosed on TikTok.
                - Cut hard on the last syllable so the end flows into frame 0
                  (cheap; don't expect a big lift on 45 s reels).
```

**Length:** 40-60 s master (Instagram peaks at 45-60 s in a 9.8M-reel dataset; >180 s halves views). A 61-90 s variant only when the explanation genuinely needs it (suits TikTok Creator Rewards >1 min, and Facebook). Never pad; never >90 s on Instagram.

## 5. Captions, text, sound

- **Captions** burned in on every reel: 2-3 words per group (3-4 allowed for Bangla), one bold Latin sans + Hind Siliguri, **one accent colour per line**, emphasis by weight or the single accent, subtle scale/fade entry. English trading terms stay in Latin script (house rule). **Retire:** per-word shake, inline emoji, 3+ colours, neon primary text, yellow ALL-CAPS Hormozi look (now reads as a template).
- **Extra on-screen text** only for the hook line, key numbers and the risk line. Don't stack headline overlays over continuous speech (overlays: -6 % retention at 10 s on speech reels, Emplifi).
- **SFX:** one sound per meaningful visual event, matched to its motion, family varied. The full 4-layer stack (air-suck riser + UI click + whoosh + sub boom) only on 2-3 moments per reel. No same-whoosh-on-every-cut.
- **Music:** voice leads; bed measured 10 LU under voice (`mix_levels`), hand-duck around quiet words. Don't chase trending audio on speech reels (no reliable evidence it helps).
- **Search:** say AND show the key English terms (trading, stop loss, risk reward, crypto). Post caption: 1-2 keyword sentences, ≤5 hashtags, no links on Facebook, no ALL CAPS.
